# Run a mock interview

Read [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [interview guidance](../../guides/interviews.md), and any role prep and confirmed stories. This skill is practice, not covert assistance during an actual interview.

Agree on role/stage, format, duration, difficulty, feedback timing, and permitted tools. If there is no role, choose a generic exercise with the candidate and label it as such. Ask whether notes should be saved.

Ask one question and wait for the candidate's answer. Don't prefill their story or show an ideal answer first. Follow up on their reasoning, ownership, evidence, or a relevant changed requirement. Adapt to what they actually said instead of reading an exhaustive questionnaire.

For technical practice, agree on requirements and completion criteria before implementation. For behavioral practice, distinguish vague communication from missing evidence. For portfolio practice, test attribution and decisions, not confidential content. Keep the session within the agreed budget.

At the agreed checkpoint, give specific observed strengths, unclear or unsupported points, and two or three improvements. A score is optional only if the candidate requests a clearly defined practice rubric, never a prediction of employer success. Don't claim to know the employer's rubric.

If saving was agreed, use [mock-feedback.md](../../templates/mock-feedback.md) under the role directory or `research/practice/`. Confirm newly surfaced facts before changing the career inventory or story bank. End with an optional next exercise. A mock never changes application or interview status.

## Session checkpoint

Before stopping or changing workflows, save the canonical files and update `session.md` under the selected private workspace using the checkpoint contract in `AGENTS.md`. Record the pending candidate decision and checks actually performed. Honor declined storage, and don't mark a task complete merely because a draft exists.
