# Prepare negotiation and decisions

## Inputs and mode

Read [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [negotiation guidance](../../guides/negotiation.md), the candidate's search preferences, tracker, and relevant role/debrief files. Ask whether this is early preparation, a verbal discussion, a written offer, or hypothetical practice. Don't require an offer just to prepare questions, and don't turn a hypothetical into an actual package.

## Procedure

1. Ask privately about priorities, constraints, acceptable alternatives, and genuine options. The candidate can keep a floor out of files. Don't infer a walk-away threshold from search preferences or select a target for them.
2. Gather actual terms and source/date using [offer.md](../../templates/offer.md). Separate formal terms, verbal statements, published ranges, and unknowns. Don't invent a competing offer, deadline, or negotiating authority.
3. Clarify currency, period, base, guaranteed versus target bonus, signing timing/clawback, equity instrument/units/vesting, benefits, scope, location, hours, and contingencies. Material unknowns become questions before a counteroffer.
4. Compare first-year and recurring cash separately. Don't double-count a guarantee inside a target bonus, a one-time payment, or a multi-year grant. Model real vesting and private-equity liquidity uncertainty. Show arithmetic and assumptions, including exchange rate/date if used. Avoid net-pay claims without a qualified model.
5. For private equity, follow the guide's modeling section: value options at the spread over the strike using both the preferred price and the 409A, include dilution and liquidation preferences, weight exit scenarios explicitly with a no-liquidity case, and separate a two-year cash view from the four-year probability-weighted value. When only an option count is known, label any inferred share count or price as an estimate and list the recruiter questions that would replace it. At companies that pay by level, raise leveling before negotiating within a band.
6. If market research is useful, use dated sources for comparable role, level, location, currency, and compensation components. Record limitations. Crowdsourced total compensation isn't guaranteed cash or evidence of what this employer will offer.
7. Prepare a few prioritized asks with reasons, alternatives, timing, and a concise email or call outline. Include non-pay terms where they matter. Keep private floors and undisclosed processes out of the draft. Never bluff.
8. Compare accepting, negotiating, or declining against the candidate's criteria. Flag material legal, immigration, and tax questions for qualified advice. Request revised written terms before treating a discussed change as settled.

## Completion

Save private preparation or comparison notes in the role directory, or a clearly private cross-offer comparison under `applications/`. Return assumptions, questions, asks, and draft paths. The candidate decides what to communicate or accept and handles sending, accepting, rejecting, or resigning themselves. Don't perform those actions for them. Update outcome status only after they confirm their decision.

## Session checkpoint

Before stopping or changing workflows, save the canonical files and update `session.md` under the selected private workspace using the checkpoint contract in `AGENTS.md`. Record the pending candidate decision and checks actually performed. Honor declined storage, and don't mark a task complete merely because a draft exists.
