# Career evidence and claim boundaries

Candidate confirmation is a source, not independent verification. Unknown is different from absent. Keep confidential details out and use redacted descriptions where necessary.

## Career inventory

Include education or certifications only if applicable and confirmed. Record a career gap or transition only to the extent the candidate wants help discussing it.
