## Tradeoffs

What would make a lower-paying, different-level, less familiar, or differently located role worthwhile? Ask only relevant questions. Don't assign numeric weights unless the candidate wants them.

## Sensitive fields

Work authorization, sponsorship, demographic selections, legal answers, and private compensation thresholds may remain candidate-only. Don't infer them from location or prior employment. Record only what the candidate chooses to store.
