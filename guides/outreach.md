# Useful outreach without pressure

Start from an existing relationship or a specific reason to contact someone. Ask for the recipient, channel, relationship, desired next step, and whether the candidate has already contacted the company.

A referral request should make the relevant work and fit easy to understand, include the exact role link, and give the recipient a comfortable way to decline. Don't imply a relationship or endorsement that doesn't exist.

A recruiter follow-up should state what actually happened, provide the relevant update or question, and avoid repeatedly contacting someone without a reason. Confirm attachments and dates before mentioning them. No response doesn't establish rejection.

Prepare a short draft in the candidate's voice. Keep private negotiation thresholds and other processes out unless the candidate chooses to disclose them. Don't scrape private contact details, mass-message people, send automatically, or treat a prepared draft as delivered.
